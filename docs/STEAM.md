## 名称 Title
秒针 SECOND HAND

## 一句话 Short description (≤ 300 chars)
**中文：** 回溯三秒——受过的伤撤销，打出的伤保留，而三秒前的你会继续并肩作战。在停摆于 11:59:59 的钟表之城里，攀上钟塔，让最后一秒走完。

**English:** Rewind three seconds — the damage you took is undone, the damage you dealt is kept, and your past self fights on beside you. Climb a clock tower frozen at 11:59:59 and let the city's last second end.

## 关于 About
**撤销失误，保留胜果。**

你是滴答——大钟上那根活过来的秒针。整座城停在了午夜前的最后一秒，只有你还在走。

- **回溯三秒**：随时倒回三秒前的位置，撤销这段时间受到的伤害。
- **与过去的自己并肩**：回溯后，一道"残响"会原样重演你刚才的每一刀。打得越好，残响越强。
- **悖论**：主动撞向自己的残响，引发大范围时间爆炸。残响的未来路径清晰可见——为交汇点做好规划。
- **为回溯而设计的首领**：会撤销你伤害的档案管理员；让房间陷入黑暗、只有残响能照亮的灯蛾女王；能让时间停止、只有残响还能行动的"静止"。
- **五位守护者，五十余段回忆**：灼烧、齿轮造物、共鸣、锈蚀、定格——每一段回忆都在改写你和过去自己的协作方式。
- **四把武器，各有残响特性**，三十余个零件，沙漏强化，双重回忆。
- **手工像素美术**，动态光照，自适应音乐，完整中英双语，Xbox 手柄支持。

## Key features (English)
- **Rewind is your defence.** Fly back three seconds and undo the hits you took. Aggressive play is rewarded, not punished.
- **Your past fights with you.** Every rewind leaves an Echo that replays your last three seconds of attacks.
- **Paradox.** Crash into your own Echo for a time-shattering explosion — its future path is drawn on the floor so you can plan the collision.
- **Bosses that bend time back**: one who revises your damage, one who plunges the room into darkness lit only by your echoes, and one who stops time itself.
- **Deep builds**: 5 patrons, 55 memories including duos, 31 parts, rewind upgrades, weapon calibrations.
- **4 weapons**, each with its own echo trait.
- Hand-crafted pixel art, dynamic lighting, an adaptive soundtrack, full English & Simplified Chinese, Xbox controller support.
